How to Play Target Ninja

🥷 Objective

Combine all given number cards using (+, −, ×, ÷) to match the target goal exactly!

🕹️ How to make moves:

  1. Select a Number Card.
  2. Select an Operator (+, −, ×, ÷).
  3. Select a second Number Card to perform math!
  4. The cards will combine into a single new card.
  5. Continue combining until 1 card remains matching the goal!

🍕 Keeping your streak:

Your streak grows with every solve. It resets if you skip a puzzle with Next, reveal the Solution, or use a Strategic Hint. Leonardo's free hint and the Smoke Bomb keep your streak safe!
