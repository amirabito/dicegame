How to Play Target Ninja

🥷 Objective

Combine all the number cards using +, −, ×, and ÷ to make the target number exactly!

🕹️ How to make moves:

  1. Select a Number Card.
  2. Select an Operator (+, −, ×, ÷).
  3. Select a second Number Card to do the math!
  4. The two cards combine into one new card.
  5. Keep combining until 1 card is left that matches the target!

🔢 Whole Numbers Only

When this is ON, every step must give a whole number (0, 1, 2, 3…). No fractions or negative numbers. Turn it OFF for a bigger challenge.

💡 Hints come in 3 steps

First a nudge about which operation to try, then the first move, then the full answer. Only ask for as much help as you need!

🍕 Keeping your streak

Your streak grows with every solve. It resets if you skip a puzzle with Next, reveal the Solution, or use a Strategic Hint. Kai's free hint and the Smoke Bomb keep your streak safe!

⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts

Press 1–5 to pick a card, + − * / for operators, U to undo, and Esc to close a window.

📺 Projector Mode

Tap the button for giant cards and a two-team scoreboard. Great for whole-class warm-ups!
